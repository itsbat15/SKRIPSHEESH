ABSTRAK 
 
Bimbingan Belajar Artha Balikpapan merupakan lembaga pendidikan 
nonformal yang mengelola delapan cabang aktif dengan 60 tentor dan sekitar 
2.400 siswa/i di Kota Balikpapan. Pengelolaan operasional lembaga saat ini masih 
mengandalkan proses manual melalui formulir kertas, buku besar fisik, serta 
penagihan SPP melalui WhatsApp dengan penelusuran nomor kontak satu per 
satu. Kondisi tersebut memicu tingginya beban kerja staf administrasi, risiko 
selisih perhitungan kas harian, lambatnya identifikasi tunggakan siswa, serta 
ketiadaan konsolidasi laporan keuangan terpusat bagi pemilik lembaga. Penelitian 
ini bertujuan merancang dan membangun prototipe sistem informasi manajemen 
operasional bimbingan belajar multicabang berbasis web untuk mendigitalisasi 
empat fungsi krusial, yaitu pendaftaran siswa baru, pencatatan transaksi, verifikasi 
bukti transfer, dan rekonsiliasi kas harian. Perancangan sistem dimodelkan 
menggunakan Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN), Use Case 
Diagram, Activity Diagram, dan Entity Relationship Diagram (ERD). 
Pengembangan perangkat lunak menerapkan metode Personal Extreme 
Programming (PXP) yang adaptif bagi pengembang mandiri melalui perulangan 
iteratif. Fitur utama yang dikembangkan mencakup kewenangan hak akses data, 
batas jatuh tempo tagihan disertai pesan pengingat WhatsApp, validasi bukti 
transfer bank, serta konsolidasi kas antarcabang secara real-time. Evaluasi 
kelayakan sistem dirancang melalui Unit Testing, Black Box Testing untuk 
memverifikasi fungsionalitas logika, serta User Acceptance Testing (UAT) 
bersama pihak pemilik lembaga dan staf administrasi cabang. Implementasi 
prototipe ini diharapkan mampu meningkatkan efisiensi kerja administrasi serta

DESIGN AND DEVELOPMENT OF A WEB-BASED 
OPERATIONAL MANAGEMENT INFORMATION SYSTEM FOR 
A TUTORING CENTER USING THE PERSONAL EXTREME 
PROGRAMMING METHOD (CASE STUDY: BIMBEL ARTHA 
BALIKPAPAN) 
 
 
By                                   : 
Ariel Itsbat Nurhaq 
Studen Identity Number :  
10231018 
Supervisor                      : 
Arif Wicaksono Septyanto, M.Kom. 
Co- Supervisor               : 
Sri Rahayu Natasia, S.Kom., M.Si., M.Sc. 
 
ABSTRACT 
 
Bimbingan Belajar Artha Balikpapan is a non-formal educational 
institution managing eight active branches with 60 tutors and approximately 
2,400 students in Balikpapan City. The institution's operational management 
currently relies on manual processes involving paper forms, physical ledgers, and 
tuition fee collection via WhatsApp through manual individual contact searches. 
These conditions cause a heavy administrative workload, risks of daily cash 
calculation discrepancies, delayed identification of overdue tuition payments, and 
the lack of centralized financial reporting for the institution's owner. This study 
aims to design and develop a web-based multi-branch operational management 
information system prototype to digitalize four crucial functions: new student 
registration, transaction recording, payment transfer proof verification, and daily 
cash reconciliation. System design is modeled using Business Process Model and 
Notation (BPMN), Use Case Diagrams, Activity Diagrams, and Entity 
Relationship Diagrams (ERD). Software development applies the Personal 
Extreme Programming (PXP) methodology, which is adaptive for solo developers 
through iterative cycles. Key features developed include role-based data access 
control, payment due date determination with structured WhatsApp reminder 
messages, bank transfer proof validation, and real-time inter-branch cash 
consolidation. System evaluation is designed through Unit Testing, Black Box 
Testing to verify functional logic, and User Acceptance Testing (UAT) conducted 
with the institution owner and branch administrative staff. The implementation of
